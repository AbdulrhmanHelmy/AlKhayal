# Cash sales and shift revenue

Completing a cash sale in the touch POS now deposits the exact final invoice total in the cashbox `إيراد الوردية`. The common Sales posting boundary applies the same rule when that cash invoice is posted from the standard editor/API. A credit sale creates customer debt and no treasury receipt. Existing posted sales are not charged/deposited retroactively.

## Identity and posting

Application `ShiftRevenuePolicy` defines the cashbox name/code and recognizes the operator spelling `ايراد الورديه` (hamza/ta marbuta variations). On the first positive cash posting, the SQLite writer reuses a single visible cashbox with that name or creates `SHIFT-REVENUE`. Multiple visible matching accounts, a matching bank or an unrelated account occupying the reserved code produces an actionable conflict. No existing balance/account is overwritten. A single immutable `ShiftRevenueTreasury` row retains the chosen account identity across later postings/restarts/metadata renames. Treasury rules already prohibit changing the type or deleting an account with movements.

The sale's writer transaction includes FIFO/stock issue, cash payment, cashbox creation/link when needed, treasury deposit/audit/version, immutable invoice-to-movement source link and invoice posting/audit. A conflict, overflow or late failure rolls back every change. Retry with the same sale operation key replays the posted invoice and source IDs without another deposit; changed request/actor or reuse by another invoice conflicts. Treasury keys are namespaced `sale-revenue:` and cannot silently claim unrelated movements. New zero-net sales remain rejected by the existing Application validation; defensively, a legacy zero-total draft has no treasury deposit.

Additive migration `029_sales_treasury.sql` creates `ShiftRevenueTreasury` and `SalesTreasuryReceipts` with foreign keys, unique movement/invoice identity, an account lookup index and update/delete guards. It inserts no account or financial history during migration. Historical posted invoices retain no new treasury source link.

`GET /api/sales`, invoice GET and POST-to-`/post` responses include nullable `treasuryAccountId`, `treasuryAccountName` and `treasuryMovementId`. The touch POS explains the automatic destination before checkout and confirms the saved receipt afterward. Existing session/CSRF and Sales/POS screen authorization protect posting. Cashiers need no manual treasury deposit or account-management grant; those permissions remain separate. Treasury validation failures return a `treasury_*` conflict through the existing sales result envelope.

The name represents one shared cashbox; shift opening/closing, per-cashier balances and shift reconciliation are not implemented by this change. Sales cash refunds now withdraw solely from مرتجعات البيع and leave this cashbox unchanged; see [returns-management.md](returns-management.md). Later credit-invoice collections retain their existing independent funding boundary. Cash cancellation remains blocked by the existing refund-policy rule.
