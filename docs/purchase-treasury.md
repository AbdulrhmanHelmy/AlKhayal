# Cash purchase payment account

Purchase Management requires an explicit cashbox/bank selection when `paymentMode` is `cash`. The selector displays saved account names, type and exact current balance. It appears immediately when switching to cash and clears/hides when switching to credit. A cash draft cannot be saved without a valid account. Changing the payment method/account on a saved draft requires saving before posting, so the operator cannot accidentally post against the previous account. Successful draft save releases the busy guard and retains editable line metadata, allowing repeated draft saves.

`PurchaseDraftCommand`, API request and `PurchaseInvoice` include nullable `treasuryAccountId`. Read invoices also include `treasuryAccountName` and nullable `treasuryMovementId`. Application validates cash/credit selection shape, and Data validates the chosen account against the transactional snapshot at draft save/post. `purchase-management` grants account lookup for this workflow and authorizes payment through purchase posting; it does not grant manual treasury withdrawal, deposit or CRUD permissions.

Migration `025_purchase_treasury.sql` additively adds the two nullable foreign keys to `PurchaseInvoices` plus a unique movement-source index. Existing drafts and posted invoices retain their IDs, amounts, status, hashes and balances. There is no retroactive deduction. An older cash draft with no account must be edited/saved with an account before posting; historical posted cash invoices retain their existing replay behavior. All previous migration scripts remain unchanged, including deployed-019 compatibility during pending 024.

Saving a draft never moves cash. Posting withdraws the full exact invoice total from the selected account using `SqliteTreasuryStore.PostWithinTransactionAsync`, the same ledger-writing implementation as manual treasury operations. That method uses the existing purchase connection/writer transaction and never commits independently. The immutable movement, treasury version/audit, stock receipt/FIFO, purchase source links/status/audit and supplier purchase/payment ledger entries all commit together or roll back together. Missing/deleted accounts and insufficient balance reject posting with a `treasury_*` conflict. Monetary policy remains scale 4 with piastre-finalized invoice totals; no JavaScript money arithmetic controls the withdrawal.

The invoice records the resulting movement ID, and the unique index prevents multiple invoices from owning one withdrawal. The server creates `purchase-treasury:{invoiceId}` as the stable movement operation key and binds its hash to the purchase request. Purchase hashes include the selected account only when present, preserving historical account-less invoice hashes. Ordinary and concurrent replay return the same posted invoice/movement without additional cash, stock or supplier entries. Changing a posted invoice/account remains forbidden.

Credit purchases create no treasury movement. Supplier payments now have their own atomic treasury integration and invoice allocation, described in [supplier-payment-treasury.md](supplier-payment-treasury.md). Expense vouchers have their own atomic treasury integration in [expenses.md](expenses.md). Purchase-return cash refunds now deposit in مرتجعات الشراء; sales cash refunds withdraw solely from مرتجعات البيع. See [returns-management.md](returns-management.md). Other sales payments keep their documented accounting boundaries; this change integrates cash purchase posting only.

## Verification

`PurchasingTreasuryTests` extends the disposable inventory fixture for cash/bank selection persistence, no cash movement at draft save, exact funds/stock/FIFO/supplier reconciliation, concurrent same-invoice replay, untouched other accounts, missing/invalid accounts, insufficient balance, old account-less cash draft rejection, cash-account edits, cash-to-credit changes and injected late-audit failure rollback/retry. Existing cash-purchase financial datasets now fund/select an explicit account. Historical fixture builders exclude dependent migration 025 where purchase tables are intentionally absent; final migration expectations advance to 25.

`TreasuryApiTests` exercises a cashier granted only Purchase Management: account lookup and persisted cash purchase save/post/replay succeed, while manual withdrawal and CSRF-free purchase creation remain forbidden. `frontend/tests/purchasing-tests.js` executes the real selector handlers, explicit selection validation, exact submitted account ID, save busy-state release, retained account on redraw, save-before-post protection, and clearing the account on credit.
